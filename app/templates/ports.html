Open, closed or filtered

Handy after opening a firewall rule, forwarding a router port or starting a service. For safety only your own IP can be tested; the address is taken from your connection and cannot be changed.

Open means a service accepted the connection. Closed means the machine answered that nothing listens on that port. Filtered means no answer within 3 seconds, which is what a firewall that silently drops packets looks like. The test runs from our Noida server over IPv4 or IPv6, whichever your connection uses.

Running a service on a cloud server? Open only the ports you need and keep SSH and database ports restricted. To check your server's name resolution use the DNS propagation checker, and to see the route to it use the looking glass.
