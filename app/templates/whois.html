Reading the result

Status values such as clientTransferProhibited are registrar locks that protect a domain against unauthorised transfer. The expiry date tells you when to renew; the nameservers show where the DNS is hosted. Personal contact data is redacted by registries and never shown here.

Moving a site? After you change nameservers, confirm the change with the DNS propagation checker, then host it on a cloud server in India. For an IP, also try the reverse DNS lookup.
