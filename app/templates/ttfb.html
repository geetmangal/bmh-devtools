TTFB & SSL Checker

Measure how fast a website responds, phase by phase: DNS lookup, TCP connect, TLS handshake, time to first byte (TTFB) and download, with redirects followed up to 5 hops and 3 samples for a stable TTFB. The SSL section inspects the certificate chain, expiry, SAN list, TLS version, cipher, HSTS and HTTP/2 support. Measured from a BookMyHost server in India.

Reading the waterfall

DNS is the time to translate the hostname into an IP address. TCP is one network round trip to open the connection; it is a pure measure of distance and routing between this server and yours. TLS is the handshake (one round trip with TLS 1.3, two with TLS 1.2). TTFB is the wait for the first response byte after the request is sent: this is where slow databases, uncached pages and overloaded origins show up. Download is the remaining transfer, capped here at 1 MiB.

Hosting the site close to its visitors removes most of the TCP and TLS cost. For Indian audiences, a server in India such as a BookMyHost cloud VPS typically cuts 150 to 250 ms per round trip compared with a European or US origin.
