Looking Glass: Ping, Traceroute & BGP

Run ping and traceroute from our network in Noida, India (AS152523) to any public IP address or hostname, and look up the BGP prefix, origin AS and sample AS paths for the target.







  
What a looking glass tells you

  
A looking glass shows how the internet looks from another vantage point. Ping and traceroute here start inside a BookMyHost data centre in Noida, so the latency and path are what a visitor on an Indian network sees when reaching your server. The BGP view uses public RIPEstat data (RIPE NCC route collectors), not our own routers.

  
Planning a move? Compare the round trip to your current host with a cloud server in India, or measure your own browser latency with the Noida latency test. Also see the DNS propagation checker and the TTFB and SSL checker.
