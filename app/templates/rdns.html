PTR records and email delivery

Forward-confirmed reverse DNS (FCrDNS) means the hostname must resolve back to the same IP. Mail servers use this check when deciding whether to accept your email.

A PTR record is the reverse of an A or AAAA record. It is published in the in-addr.arpa or ip6.arpa zone by whoever owns the address block, so on a VPS you set it in the control panel or through your provider. For a mail server, use the same hostname as the SMTP HELO name and make sure its A record points to the IP.
