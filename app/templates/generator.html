About this generator

SPF lists the servers allowed to send mail for your domain and DMARC tells receivers what to do when a message fails. This page only builds the text of the two DNS records; publish them at your DNS provider and then verify them with the SPF and DMARC checkers.

The DNS lookup count is an estimate: every include, a and mx costs one lookup here, but the records you include can contain more includes of their own, so the real number can be higher.
